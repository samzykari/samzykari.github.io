Nolay Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 28, 2026

Welcome to Nolay. We built this product with a fundamental belief: your schedule, your routines, and your time belong to you. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Nolay website page.

Because Nolay is designed with a local-first architecture, the vast majority of your data is handled on your device in the app experience. On the Nolay website page, only data related to website interactions and submissions is processed as described below.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information Stored Locally (Guest Mode)

When you use Nolay in Guest Mode, your tasks, routines, calendar events, and schedules (your "User Content") are stored strictly locally in a database on your device. We do not have access to your local User Content, nor do we store it on any cloud servers.

B. Account Information and Feedback Submissions (Website)

The Nolay website page does not currently provide account creation or sign-in forms for end users. If you submit feedback or an issue through the page, the site may create an anonymous Firebase authentication session in the background to process your submission safely. In that case, we process:

  • An anonymous Firebase user ID.
  • The name you optionally provide in the form.
  • Your feedback or issue content, title, rating (if provided), and submission timestamp.

C. Diagnostic and Usage Data

The current Nolay website page does not include Firebase Crashlytics. At this time, diagnostic and usage processing for this page is limited to standard browser/network telemetry and Firebase service-level operational logs needed to deliver form submissions.

We do not intentionally collect your personal task or schedule data through this website page.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Nolay website page.
  • Authenticate anonymous website submission sessions where needed.
  • Store and display submitted website feedback and issues.
  • Respond to any customer support inquiries you initiate.

We do not use your data for advertising, and there are no ads in Nolay.

3. Third-Party Services & Integrations

Nolay integrates with specific third-party services to provide core functionality. You are subject to their respective privacy policies when utilizing these features:

  • Google Drive (User-Initiated Backups): Nolay offers an optional feature to back up your local database to your personal Google Drive. If you enable this, Nolay requests permission to write data to a specific app folder in your Drive. Nolay cannot read or access your personal files or photos on Google Drive. We do not touch, intercept, or route your backups through our own servers.
  • Firebase / Google Cloud: We use Google's Firebase platform for anonymous authentication and Cloud Firestore storage of submitted feedback/issues from this website page. You can read Google's Privacy Policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Third-Party AI (BYO-AI): Nolay features a "Bring Your Own AI" import system that parses plain-text outputs generated by third-party AIs (such as ChatGPT, Claude, and others). Because you copy and paste this text directly into Nolay, Nolay does not send your data to these AI providers behind your back.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

  • Service Providers: With Google/Firebase, to facilitate anonymous authentication and storage of website submissions.
  • Legal Requirements: If required to do so by law, court order, or governmental request.

5. Data Security & Retention

Security

For the Nolay website page, we use industry-standard transport security and platform safeguards for authenticated requests and stored submissions. For local schedule data in the Nolay app experience, security primarily depends on your device's security (for example, passcode or biometric lock).

Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: You can delete your locally stored tasks and schedules at any time simply by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's local storage.
  • Cloud Backups: You can delete your Google Drive backups directly through your Google Drive account console.
  • Account Data: If you created an account, you can request the deletion of your account and associated email address by contacting us (see below) or navigating to the account deletion option within the app's settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Nolay does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under age 13 without verification of parental consent, we will delete that information as quickly as possible.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and publishing the updated version on the Nolay website page.
