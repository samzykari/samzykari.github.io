Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 28, 2026

1. What This Policy Covers

This policy explains how information is handled on the SAMZYKARI website and applies to the features currently available on this domain.

2. Information Collected

Depending on how you use the site, the following data may be processed:

  • Account and sign-in data through Firebase Authentication (Google sign-in or email/password sign-up and login).
  • Basic profile data returned by authentication providers, such as display name, email, user ID, and profile image URL.
  • Public feedback/log submissions you choose to post (message text, type/category, display name, user ID, timestamp).
  • Usage analytics events (for example, page views, login method events, and feedback submission events) through Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Local browser storage values used for UI/session behavior (for example, launch animation state and preferences).

3. How Information Is Used

  • To provide sign-in and account-related functionality.
  • To display your selected profile details in the site UI after authentication.
  • To store, retrieve, and render feedback/log messages in site sections where posting is enabled.
  • To measure and improve site performance, content, and user experience.
  • To operate core UI features such as one-time launch/animation behavior.

4. Data Storage and Third Parties

This site uses Google Firebase services, including Authentication, Firestore Database, and Analytics. Data you provide or generate through site interactions may be processed and stored by these services according to Google/Firebase terms and policies.

5. Public Content Notice

Messages posted to feedback/log areas are intended to be displayed in the site interface. Do not submit sensitive personal information in feedback content.

6. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used through platform services, but no internet transmission or storage system is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

7. Your Choices

  • You may choose not to authenticate; some features will be unavailable.
  • You may sign out at any time.
  • You can clear browser storage and cookies through your browser settings.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as website features evolve. Any changes become effective when posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact options provided on the Communicate page.
