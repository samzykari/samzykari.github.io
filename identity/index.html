ELECTRONICS

I kind of see this as the same thing as computers. They’re both tech, right?
I’d like to say I don’t do as much of it now because school keeps me busy… but honestly that’s probably just a fancy way of saying I got a little lazy.

Back then though, I was obsessed. I bought my first soldering iron in 2017 when I was still a kid. Relax -- stop trying to estimate my age.

No one in my family or around me knew anything about electronics. It all started from those OG YouTube DIY videos. One video led to curiosity, curiosity led to research, and somehow I ended up reading about transistors, capacitors, and resistors at an age where my friends would just stare at me like, “What is this guy even talking about?”

I still remember the days when nobody trusted me with electronics. Mostly because I had a habit of taking things apart just to understand them… and sometimes ruining it while putting it back together. Other times it meant sacrificing some rarely-used device just so I could steal the motor for an RC project.

Those were the days.

I kept learning though, and now I’d say I’m pretty comfortable with whatever electronics you throw at me. I still build things once in a while. I’ve got a full soldering kit and some tools in my setup… just not quite the same level of chaos as before.

STAY CURIOUS

I know I’m not exactly successful enough to be giving advice. Actually, who am I kidding… I’m not even successful yet.

But if I had to say one thing, it would be this: do what excites you and stay curious.

Most of the things I know today started with simple curiosity. Before I got into electronics, I was into reading, writing, and a bunch of other things I won’t bore you with. But I’m pretty sure if I had stuck with those paths, I wouldn’t be as happy as I am now.
Not that everything is perfect — every path has its ups and downs.

Still, I’d rather deal with the worst parts of this than do anything else in the world.

Also, don’t be lazy. Unlike me. Get your life together.

…Okay, that part might have just been to make you feel better. Or maybe it wasn’t.
